{{- /* Secondary-structure motifs that draw themselves in the clear band below the artwork. The shapes are not synthesised. They are connected components lifted straight out of the cover image — a flood fill over the artwork's own 7px cell grid found 209 structures, and these are three of the cleanest: two α-helices and a β-strand. Drawing them by hand kept failing because a smooth curve next to pixel art reads as a foreign object, and a hand-built pixel helix loses the occlusion between turns that makes a coil legible. Reusing the real cells sidesteps both: the geometry is right because it is the same geometry. Cells are stamped at the artwork's own pitch and square size, then lifted in value — these sit on bare background rather than among other structures, so they have to carry on their own. Widths are percentages of the band and the height comes from the image's own ratio, so a motif tracks the artwork as it scales instead of drifting off its pitch. `image-rendering: pixelated` keeps the cell edges hard. Tunables live on `.fx-chain` in css/main.css (§ cover). */ -}} {{- $motifs := slice (dict "name" "helix-a" "pos" "--l: 5%" "style" "--b: 8%; --w: 9.5%; --d: 19s; --dl: -2s") (dict "name" "strand" "pos" "--l: 45%" "style" "--b: 5%; --w: 5.0%; --d: 23s; --dl: -13s") (dict "name" "helix-b" "pos" "--r: 6%" "style" "--b: 6%; --w: 9.8%; --d: 29s; --dl: -22s") -}}
