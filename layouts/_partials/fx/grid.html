{{- /* Flickering grid: scattered squares lighting up and going out, on the same cell pitch as the cover artwork. The artwork is pixel art — 5–6px squares on a 7px pitch in the source, about 4.6px on screen at a 1440px-wide band. Matching that pitch is what makes the flicker read as part of the same image rather than an overlay, and the palette is sampled from the structures themselves (all pure cyan-to-blue, R = 0). A full grid at that pitch would be ~27,000 cells, of which a couple of dozen are lit at any instant. Only the candidates are emitted; the result on screen is identical. Positions come from a seeded shuffle so they are stable between builds, and cycles are spread over 7–17s with random negative delays, so the field never pulses in unison. Tunables live on `.fx-grid` in css/main.css (§ cover). */ -}}
